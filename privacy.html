FoldPine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

FoldPine ("the app"), published by Sycamore Hill Studios, is a local-only app.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no analytics code and no advertising SDK.
  • Your PDFs stay on your device. The app only opens files you pick through Android's own file picker, processes them entirely on your device, and saves results only where you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the app has no server.
  • Passwords. If you add or remove a PDF password, the password is used on-device for that one operation and is never stored or transmitted.
  • No accounts. The app requires no sign-up.
  • Purchases. Optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The app never sees or stores your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Files you saved to your own folders remain yours, untouched.
